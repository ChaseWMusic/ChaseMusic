Cancellation Policy.

Payment

Weekly lessons are paid at the time of the lesson. Monthly packages are paid in full upfront.

About me.

Hi, I'm Chase, a Berklee-trained multi-instrumentalist teaching private music lessons right here in Princeton. Whether you're just starting out or picking a song apart note by note, I build every lesson around where you actually are, not a one-size-fits-all curriculum. My goal is simple: make lessons a place kids and adults alike genuinely want to show up to.

Good to know.

What ages do you teach?

Ages 5 and up, kids and adults alike. Complete beginners are welcome, and so are players picking an instrument back up.

What instruments do you teach?

Guitar, bass, and drums, for kids and adults, from first-timers to students ready to dig into music theory.

How does the free trial work?

It's a free 45-minute session with no obligation to continue. We'll talk about your goals and experience, then work through a few beginner-friendly fundamentals so you can feel how I teach.

Where are lessons held?

Lessons are taught from a home studio near Princeton, NJ. Zoom is available if you'd rather learn from home.

Do you offer Zoom lessons?

Yes, for every instrument except drums, which are taught in person only.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Cancel before that window and we'll set up a make-up lesson at no extra charge. Cancellations or missed lessons within 24 hours are charged the full fee.

How do I sign up?

Call or email me with who the lessons are for, which instrument you want to learn, and a few times that work. I'll get back to you to set up your free trial.
